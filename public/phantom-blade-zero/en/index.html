Frequently asked questions

Which collectibles can I check?

Bells, amber, blackstone, and other collectibles. Filter by map or category to view locations and related quest conditions.

Do I need to load a save?

No. You can browse the checklist and locations directly. Load a .sav file to check your own missing entries.

Will my save be uploaded or modified?

No. Your file is read and analyzed in your current browser. The tool does not upload files or modify your original save.

Help

  1. Load a .sav file

    Choose or drop your game save. It is analyzed in your browser, never uploaded, and never modified.

  2. Choose a save slot and map

    Choose the slot to check, then filter by map, category, or name. Select “Missing / unverified only” to narrow the results.

  3. View locations and conditions

    Select an item or marker to see its location, nearby bells, and collection conditions. “Unverified” means the evidence is insufficient; check your game.

Load your save again after refreshing. CSV export includes the current filtered results. Basic visitor statistics do not include your save content or search terms.
